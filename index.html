PulseWalk Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 (v2)

PulseWalk ("the app") is published by Synergy ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information stored only on your phone

  • Profile: your name, weight, height, weekly goal and emergency contact. Used to calculate calories and BMI and for the SOS feature.
  • Walk history: distance, time, calories and the route of each walk you record.

This information stays on your device. We do not upload it to our servers.

2. Location

PulseWalk uses your device's precise location only while you are recording a walk, to measure distance and draw your route. During a walk the app shows an ongoing notification. The app does not access your location in the background when you are not recording a walk.

2b. Steps, water and Health Connect (optional)

  • Step counter: if you allow "Physical activity", the app reads your phone's step sensor to show today's steps. Step counts stay on your phone.
  • Water log and reminders: the glasses you log stay on your phone. Reminders are local notifications.
  • Health Connect: if you choose to connect, PulseWalk writes your finished walks (exercise session, distance and active calories) to Health Connect and reads today's step count so steps from your watch or other apps can be shown. Data read from Health Connect is only displayed on your phone; it is never uploaded to our servers, shared with others, or used for advertising. You can remove access any time in the Health Connect app.

PulseWalk's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Group events (optional)

If you choose to join or create an event with an event code, the following is sent to our cloud database (Google Firebase) while you walk in that event:

  • the name you enter for the event,
  • your live location, distance, walking time and status (walking, paused, finished),
  • whether you pressed the SOS button.

This information is visible only to the organizer of that event, so they can keep walkers safe. To use events, the app creates an anonymous sign-in ID with Firebase Authentication; we do not ask for your email or phone number.

4. SOS button

When you press SOS, the app alerts the event organizer (if you are in an event) and opens your SMS app with a message to your emergency contact containing a map link to your location. Nothing is sent by SMS unless you press send.

5. Finisher badge

Badges are created on your phone. They are only shared when you choose to share them.

6. Third-party services

  • Google Maps SDK for Android – to display maps.
  • Google Play services location – to get your location while recording a walk.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) – for group events.

These services are operated by Google and follow Google's Privacy Policy.

7. We do not

  • sell your data,
  • show ads,
  • use your data for advertising or tracking across other apps.

8. Health information

Calorie and BMI figures are estimates for general fitness purposes. PulseWalk is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

9. Data retention and deletion

Data on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. To delete event data stored in the cloud, email us at stunnerconsult@gmail.com with the event code and the name you used, and we will delete it within 30 days.

10. Children

PulseWalk is not directed at children under 13.

11. Security

Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit. Access to event data is restricted by security rules so only the event organizer can read walkers' locations.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page.
